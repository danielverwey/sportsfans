# Permission requests

Two rugby sources state no licence. The scores are facts, but the compilations are their authors' work, so the honest route is to ask. Both drafts below are ready to send; keep the replies in this file.

## 1 · Nuck's Rugby Archive

**Where:** open an issue at https://github.com/rugbyarchive/rugbyarchive.github.io/issues (the About page gives no email).

**Subject:** Permission to build on the archive for a non-commercial fan site

> Hello Nuck,
>
> I'm Daniel, a rugby fan in South Africa building a set of fan-made, non-commercial sports atlases at sportsfans.co.za — single pages that draw a sport's whole history and let you explore it. The rugby atlas covers ten nations (South Africa, New Zealand, Australia, Argentina, England, France, Ireland, Wales, Scotland, Italy) from 1871 to the present, and the international match results in it were imported from your archive: the fixtures marked as full internationals involving those ten, with your uncertainty flags kept.
>
> Before I make it public I'd like your permission to use the archive that way. What I can promise: the site carries no advertising, sponsorship or paywall and never will; your archive is credited by name and linked on the page itself, on the site's sources page and on every static page built from the data; nothing is presented as an official record; and if you'd rather I didn't use it, or want the credit worded differently, I'll do as you ask.
>
> If you're happy for me to go ahead, a reply saying so is all I need. And if there's a way you'd like corrections fed back, I'd be glad to.
>
> Thank you for the work that went into the archive — it is a remarkable thing.
>
> Daniel

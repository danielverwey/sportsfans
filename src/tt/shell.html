APEX / An ode to the Mountain · Isle of Man TT · 1907–2026

I · The River

A hundred and twenty years over the mountain

Every TT race ever won, flowing left to right in the colours of the winning marque — two classes on one day at St John’s in 1907, a week of ten today. For 105 of the early races the winner is all the record holds; the coverage notes say which. The wars, the foot-and-mouth year and the pandemic leave the river open where no TT ran.
